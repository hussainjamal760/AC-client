Frequently Asked Questions

Q How does the matching service work?
Simply fill out our quick form (about 60 seconds). We'll match your request with up to three licensed HVAC professionals in your area who will contact you with a free estimate. There's no obligation to hire anyone.
Q Is there a cost to use Air Conditioner Lab?
No. Our platform is completely free for business seeking estimates. We are compensated by our network of HVAC professionals for connecting them with qualified leads.
Q How do I opt out of text messages?
Reply STOP to any text message you receive from us or a matched pro. You can also email support@airconditionerlab.com with "Opt-Out" in the subject line.
Q How do I request deletion of my data?
Email support@airconditionerlab.com with your request. See our Privacy Policy for full details on your rights.
